SIGReg (Signature Regularization) Interactive Demo

Overview: SIGReg prevents representational collapse in architectures like JEPAs by forcing embedding distributions to match a standard isotropic Gaussian N(0, I). Based on the Cramér–Wold theorem, it projects high-dimensional embeddings onto random 1D directions and aligns their empirical characteristic function φ̂(t) = (1/N) Σ eitx with the target e-t²/2.
